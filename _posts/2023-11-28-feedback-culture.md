Why should you bother what other people think about you? That`s because, other people can promote you, help you to improve yourself. But, there is a difference
difference when feedback comes from your manager and from peers.

Feedback can really change how you work or how you do things in life and it's a good thing. But when feedback comes without context, when it comes as gossip, that's not professional nor it should matter to you. I`ll explain.

## Asking for feedback

I have a habit to ask for feedback from all my peers and leadership every 1 or 2 months. This give me knowledge on how I'm doing: if I'm doing things the way that is expected, if my coleagues find it nice to work with me, If I`m not desrespecting someone and etc...

That feedback sessions generally start by myself explaing why I`m in that meeting with the person, and If the person is confortable to give me some feedback, positive or negative about my work or how I relate to them. If the person is confortable, I ask if I can give some feedback to him.

I`ve being doing that for the past 5 years and that habit has given me many insights on how to improve myself, how to help my team and how to make my way to promotion and being notice by the managers. 

Some people will not be confortable with that aproach, but after some sessions thing tend to be better and share more things. I prefer to write what I'll say to each person, good or bad feedback... This gives something very important to feedback: `context`. I can not say just "you did that", that's not a good feedback, is too vague and very open do interpretation which leads to bad comunication, very unconfortable talks. So, I prefer to give the full picture. For example:

Hard skill example: 

PersonX, some of your Pull Requests comes without unit tests. I think you could give some attention to it not only to improve our code quality, but to improve your knowledge on how the system works.

Instead of:

PersonX, `all` your Pull Request comes without unit tests. You should fix that.

Soft skill example:

PersonX, in that planning last week, when we were discussing about project W you was rude with me. I was given my architect sugestion and you said that my idea was just trash, without even listening to the full context and the full idea. You abruptally interrupted me. I`m not ok with that... Can you please improve your way to comunicate in a less violent way?

Instead of:
PersonX, you was very rude with me in the planning, that's not cool.

What I'm trying to show with those examples is that feedback should be given some thought process and not just be given. If you know the person for 2 years, and one day they were rude with you, there is a BIG change that the problem is not you, but the person is passing through some kind of stress. Giving the full context not only will enable that person to know more about you, but also to they open their hearts and speak what the real problema was. This improve the relation with you.


## Giving feedback

Here we have two cases: if you area a peer or a manager.

### The Manager

As a manager you should not only give feedback, but investigate feedbacks that you hear. For example, if somene says that PersonX was rude to PersonY. Your job should be investigating why that has happen and how you can prevent from happening again.

You can do that talking with the people involved in the issue, but also understanding the moment of the team. Is there some kind of pressure to deliver a feature? Was someone fired? Is that fired person related with PersonX? 

Those questions should give a nice view on why PersonX was rude to PersonY. With that view you can provide the correct tone of the conversation. PersonX, should not be reprimanded if it was just one case, one time because of stress. She should be given some direction to correct that behavior and learn how to deal with stress. On the other hand, if PersonX was rude because She is not polite or for a child reason, then she need a more hard talk to not do that again and put her in observation for a period.

Putting people in observation is a nice way to control your feedback system, who is doing fine and who doesn`t. Consider it two lists: positive and to improve. People in the first list, are people that are close or ready to a next step in her carrer or people that you can trust with the most critical tasks. People in the other lists, you need to follow closely, making shure that they are motivated and that they are improving themselves.
